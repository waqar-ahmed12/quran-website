What to judge here

The cover parts from the centre and both leaves swing outward, pivoting on their outer edges, so the opening reads from the middle. The spread widens from one cover-width to two as they go, which is what makes it read as a book opening rather than curtains parting.

The inside of each leaf is plain endpaper — no roundel, no reversed script, nothing showing through. Scrub past about 70% to see it.

Open question: the gold roundel sits dead centre, so parting the cover divides القرآن الكريم down the middle. Fade leaves in the panel dissolves them as they swing, so it is never seen held apart. Off is the clean gatefold cut. Tell me which you want.

Dark only for now — the light pair comes after this is signed off. The aayat layer is Phase 5 and is not in here.

### Claude  ·  2026-09-14 13:32:25

All three issues are fixed. Here's what each one turned out to be:

**Aayat on the white Qur'an** — I'd left them switched off because I assumed the light book's pages sat somewhere different. **Not smooth** — two causes. The big one: 184 frames at 1920×1080 is ~57 MB to fetch and about **1.5 GB of decoded bitmaps**, four times the dark set, so the browser was discarding frames and re-decoding them mid-scroll. Now 63 frames at 1280×720 — **12.8 MB, ~230 MB decoded**, below the dark set's 320 MB. (The video's last 60 frames are just a light moving across an already-open book, so they're cut.) The second cause: my first pass measured the book by brightness, which actually tracked its *drop shadow* — and the shadow grows as the book opens, so the book drifted left and up as it went. It's now found by its sharp edge instead, reading 653 closed to 995 open.

**Theme switch losing the opening** — the handler was resetting the eased position to the raw scroll position, so the book jumped to a different point in the opening. Both takes now run the same timeline down a page of the same height, so position carries straight over, and the switch re-triggers an auto open/close the click interrupted.

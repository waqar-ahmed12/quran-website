Before and after

The teacher's 19 November recordings, each sound cut out on its own. Before is the recording as it is. After has the room's low rumble filtered out and the sharp hiss softened, only at the moments it is louder than a typical sound's. Both play at the same volume, so you compare only the sound. Removed plays only what was taken out, made 12 dB louder so you can hear it. Shaded sounds are the ones the chosen level changes; red ones are kept off the site until you have listened.

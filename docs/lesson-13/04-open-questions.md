# 04 — Open questions

Five questions, none blocking. Most of what a teacher might ask about this lesson is already asked, and answered
once, in `docs/lesson-11/07` (the Indo-Pak jazam, alternate or both, ask by ear first, "madd") and
`docs/lesson-12/07` (the Madani yaa's dots at the end). Those answers apply here unchanged.

## 0. Build after Lesson 12?

**Yes. This is a real dependency:** Lesson 12's row is this lesson's `against`.

## 1. The sample letter

| | Title and rail | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ف** — *recommended* | فِي | a real word ("in"), and the yaa joins | ف is fourth on the board, not first |
| ت | تِي | the first letter of part 1 | not a word |
| د (zair's own sample) | دِي | Lesson 5's sample | the yaa stands apart, which is the less usual picture |

## 2. The words used of Allah

Seven of the twelve reading words (رَحِيمٌ، عَلِيمٌ، سَمِيعٌ، حَكِيمٌ، عَظِيمٌ، كَرِيمٌ، قَرِيبٌ) are names the
Qur'an uses of Allah. **Recommended: keep them.** They are the long-"ee" words a student will read most, and the
page shows them as plain words, no meanings, as every reading page does. The teacher may prefer fewer, or to say
something.

## 3. "Zair and yaa" / "Kasra and yaa"

Never "madd". The mark on the Indo-Pak yaa is the student's own word, jazam or sukoon.

## 4. Words

Fifteen candidates. Three "apart" words (قَرِيبٌ، كَرِيمٌ، رِيحٌ), all after ر. The teacher may know short ones
after د ذ ز.

## 5. If the zair and the yaa collide

If `02` §4 finds a face drawing the returning yaa into the zair, the options are, in order: a different
`font-feature-settings` for the tile (turn the returning form off), then a different Madani face for the mark pages
(the options panel already switches between faces, `data-madani-font`). **Never** move the mark by hand: the fonts
place marks, and the Qaida has never positioned one itself.

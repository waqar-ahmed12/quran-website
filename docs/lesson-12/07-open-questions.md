# 07 — Open questions

Seven questions. **None blocks the build.** §1 is the one only this lesson raises.

## 0. Build after Lesson 11?

**Yes. This one is a real dependency, not a preference:** Lesson 11 opens the fourth `MARKS` statement and makes
the jazam line per-script, and this lesson's page is a copy of Lesson 11's.

## 1. The Madani yaa at the end: with dots or without?

| | Madani item | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **a. Dotted, ي** — *recommended* | بَيْ | the letter Lesson 1 taught and Lesson 3's Madani end shape; how Arabic Qaidas print their tables | the Madani mushaf writes a word-final yaa without dots (فِى) |
| b. Dotless, ى | بَىْ | the mushaf's own end shape | a letter the student has never been shown; looks like a different letter |

Indo-Pak is not in question: ی is dotless at the end by its nature, and the student has seen it in Lesson 3.

## 2. The dotless line in Indo-Pak

Say "At the end, the Indo-Pak yaa has no dots" on the board (**recommended**), or say nothing and let Lesson 3's table
have taught it? A student who skipped Lesson 3 (nothing is locked) would otherwise wonder where the dots went.

## 3. Twins: zabar and "au", or add "baa" too?

**Recommended: `['fatha', 'fatha-waw']`**, a quartet. Adding `'fatha-alif'` makes five tiles on every row, for a
contrast ("baa" against "bai") that no one confuses.

## 4. The names

"Zabar and yaa" / "Fatha and yaa". **Never "leen."** The mark on the yaa is jazam or sukoon, the student's own word.

## 5. The recordings

**27 "bai" sounds** (`fatha-yaa`), one sitting. In the order `docs/lesson-10/07` §8 suggested, they come after
Lesson 10's "bau".

## 6. Words

Fifteen candidates in `05`. Three "apart" words (رَيْبٌ، دَيْنٌ، وَيْلٌ) and one in the walkthrough (زَيْتٌ).

## 7. The fused-shape swap

If a face draws لَيْ as one shape (`02` §4), swap ل for ن in part 1 (as Lesson 8 did for لَا), or keep ل and say
nothing? **Recommended: swap**, because part 1 is where the yaa should look its ordinary self. This only arises if
the look finds a fused shape.

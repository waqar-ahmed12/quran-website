# 03 — Open questions for the whole pass

Each lesson's own questions are in its own plan. These eight are about the pass as a whole. **§1 and §2 are the
user's.** The rest are the teacher's, or can wait for the lesson that needs them.

## 1. The fifteen lessons and their order

`README.md` §2–3. **Recommended as written.** The places most worth a second opinion:
- **Al-Fatiha at 23** (before the tajweed lessons) or **at 28** (after them)? §6.
- **One lesson for noon and tanween** (24, four rules) or **two** (clear and merged, then hidden and turned)? A
  printed Qaida teaches them as one chapter. The rule page's parts (one per rule, then all) keep it finishable.
- **Heavy and light letters as one lesson** (27) or two (the seven heavy letters; then raa and Allah's laam)?

## 2. Launch after the first pass, or after this one?

| | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **After this pass** — *recommended* | the Qaida then does what the landing page says: it teaches reading the Qur'an. The first pass alone cannot read the basmala | the free Qaida waits longer |
| After the first pass | something is live sooner | a student who finishes it still cannot read one verse; the finish screen would point to lessons that do not exist yet |

## 3. The Qur'an fonts

Both are needed **by Lesson 23**, where whole verses appear (`01` §1, §9):
- **Indo-Pak**: a licensed Indo-Pak Qur'an font that matches Quran.com's Indo-Pak text (its private-use signs
  included), or the KFGQPC IndoPak font `QAIDA-CONTENT.md` already names. **Check the licence before anything else.**
- **Madani**: a face that draws Quran.com's U+0652 sukun as the Madina mushaf's open shape. The site already loads
  **Amiri Quran**, so measure that first. The King Fahd Complex's Uthmanic Hafs font is the other candidate, licence
  permitting.

**Recommended:** settle the licences during P2, while the rule lessons are being built, so Lesson 23 is not blocked
when it comes up.

## 4. Plain names or tajweed names?

| Plain (the page's title) | Tajweed (what a Qaida class says) |
|---|---|
| Said clearly / merged / turned into meem / hidden | izhar / idgham / iqlab / ikhfa |
| The hum | ghunna |
| The bounce | qalqalah |
| Heavy / light | tafkheem / tarqeeq |
| The wavy line, hold it longer | madd |
| The joining alif | hamzat al-wasl |
| The opening letters | huroof muqatta'at |

**Recommended:** plain titles and answers, with the tajweed word **named once on the board** ("this is called
*ikhfa*"). That is how Lesson 10 named "jazam" without teaching it. A student who goes on to a Qur'an teacher will
hear these words, so they should have met them. **Would the teacher rather use the tajweed words throughout?** They
are text fields either way.

## 5. Whose voice reads the verses?

Lessons 23 and 29 are whole verses. The content rules (`QAIDA-CONTENT.md`): "the user's own recordings or a vetted
reciter, never AI voice".

| | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **The teacher recites** | one voice across the whole Qaida; the teacher's own pace | about 60 verses to record |
| **A vetted reciter's teaching recitation** | made for learners (slow, clear, every rule audible). Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary's *muallim* recitation is the classic one | licence to check; a second voice |

**Recommended:** ask the teacher. If a reciter, check the licence of the specific recording before anything is
downloaded. **Nothing is downloaded without the user's yes** (the PC rules, and the site's).

## 6. Al-Fatiha before or after the tajweed lessons?

**Recommended: before (Lesson 23).** Every word of it is readable by then. What is left is the finer sound: its heavy
letters (الصِّرَاطَ, الضَّآلِّيْنَ), which the teacher's recording carries and Lesson 27 explains. Reading the surah the
student prays with, as soon as they can, is the strongest reason to keep going. The page can point forward: "the saad
here is heavy — Lesson 27".

## 7. Which surahs for Lesson 29?

**Recommended: the last ten, 105–114.** They are short, the ones most learners memorise for prayer first, and together
they use nearly every rule in this pass. Alternatives: the last twenty, or all of Juz 'Amma (37 surahs), which is too
long for one lesson and would become lessons 29–31.

## 8. How much of the first pass rides along as review?

The rule lessons can mix first-pass items into their drills at no cost (`02` §4). **Recommended: a few, off by
default**, the same as the mark lessons' bare-letter review ("don't add simple alphabets without symbols", the user,
2026-09-20). It is one row in the options panel, so the teacher can turn it on.

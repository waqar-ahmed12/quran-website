## What it teaches

Some letters are said **heavy**: the back of the tongue raised, a full, deep sound. Most are **light**. Three groups:

1. **Always heavy, the seven**: خ ص ض غ ط ق ظ (the class remembers them as خُصَّ ضَغْطٍ قِظْ). Heard best against
   their light look-alikes: **س / ص, ت / ط, ذ / ظ, د / ض, ك / ق**. Saying ص like س is the commonest mistake a new reader
   of الصِّرَاطَ makes.
2. **Raa: heavy or light by its mark.** Heavy with zabar or paish (رَبِّ, الرَّحْمٰنِ); light with zair (رِجَالٌ); with a
   jazam, it follows the vowel before it (heavy in قُرْاٰنٌ, light in فِرْعَوْنَ). The full rule has more cases, and the
   lesson teaches these three and names the rest as the teacher's.
3. **The laam of Allah.** Heavy after zabar or paish (قَالَ اللّٰهُ, هُوَ اللّٰهُ); light after zair (بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ, لِلّٰهِ).
   It is the only laam that is ever heavy.

## The two scripts

**Neither prints it.** The same rule for both, and no per-script line. This is where the Lesson 23 word notes that
said "a later lesson" are paid off (الصِّرَاطَ, الْمَغْضُوْبِ, الضَّآلِّيْنَ, the laam of اللّٰهِ).

## What is drilled

| Part | Question |
|---|---|
| 1 | the seven: "heavy or light?" for a letter alone, **pairs by ear**: hear one, pick ص or س |
| 2 | raa: "heavy or light?" by its mark |
| 3 | Allah's laam: "heavy or light?" by the vowel before it, with word pairs |
| 4 | all |

**Hear one, pick which, with look-alike pairs**, is the lesson's real question, and the one the content plan asked for
back at Lesson 11. The name-it question tests only the rule.

## Examples (from the Qur'an, by reference; candidates)

Heavy letters: الصِّرَاطَ (1:6), الْمَغْضُوْبِ (1:7), الضَّآلِّيْنَ (1:7), الْفَلَقِ (113:1). Raa: رَبِّ (1:2), الرَّحْمٰنِ (1:1).
Allah: هُوَ اللّٰهُ (112:1), بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ (1:1), لِلّٰهِ (1:2), نَصْرُ اللّٰهِ (110:1).

## What is out

The degrees of heaviness (five, by the vowel); raa's rarer cases (after a sakin yaa, and at a stop after a long
vowel); the "half-heavy" letters. All the teacher's.

## Open questions

1. **One lesson or two** (`docs/pass-2/03` §1)? **Recommended: one**, with raa and Allah as parts. They are the only
   letters whose heaviness changes.
2. **The look-alike pairs by ear** need both letters of each pair recorded alone. Lesson 1's letter names are not the
   sound. **Five pairs, ten short recordings** ("sa / sa", as the teacher says them).
3. **Plain or tajweed names** (*tafkheem* / *tarqeeq*).
